## Michael A. Princi

Michael Princi is an experienced CEO, M&A leader, and advisor with over 25 years of experience driving growth and transformation across technology, media, and industrial sectors. He has founded and scaled analytics and SaaS companies, guided organizations through complex transactions, and built systems that link strategy to measurable performance outcomes.

As Co-Founder and CEO of CerteVerus AI, he developed an analytics platform adopted by enterprise clients. At Dentsu, as Global Head of M&A, he designed and led a global framework connecting acquisitions to business performance. He has advised executives, founders, and investors on integration, digital strategy, and governance. Mike also served as Board Observer and interim CFO for Pantheon Vision, an early-stage med-tech company, supporting a $4.25M capital raise.

He is a graduate of Cornell University’s Johnson Graduate School of Management and the United States Military Academy at West Point, where he earned a B.S. in Operations Research and Systems Engineering. A former U.S. Army Ranger-qualified infantry officer, he continues to apply mission-driven leadership to business transformation.

## Richard J. Colosimo

Rick Colosimo is a multi-industry executive, attorney, and advisor with over 25 years of experience leading growth, transformation, and strategic transactions across technology, healthcare, and professional services. He has built and optimized systems connecting strategy, operations, and finance to improve enterprise performance and reduce uncertainty in future cash flows.

Rick has advised and led numerous M&A and operational initiatives, providing senior leadership as CAO, COO, and General Counsel. His recent advisory work includes engagements with HYPH, a global music technology company, and Viva Eve, a leading women’s health practice in New York City. Known for his ability to bridge strategy and execution, Rick is often the first call for founders and investors seeking practical, cross-functional insight.

He is a graduate of Cornell Law School and a former U.S. Army Ranger-qualified infantry officer, applying a disciplined, systems-based approach to business, governance, and value creation.
