There’s a common hiring quote that goes “A players hire A players; B players hire C players.” It’s been ascribed to [Steve Jobs](<https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/391717-steve-jobs-has-a-saying-that-a-players-hire-a>).

The TSC commentary to this rule: “But sometimes an A needs a job and a B is the one offering.” Just because you have someone good working for you doesn’t mean you’re an A player; maybe you got lucky. Turn ’em loose.
