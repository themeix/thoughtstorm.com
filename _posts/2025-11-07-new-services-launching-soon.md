We’re expanding our toolkit for business owners who need more than platitudes.

First up: Exit Readiness is for owners aiming to sell in 12–24 months. Not the “tidy up your financials” advice or a to-do list you can get everywhere else, but real, deliberate help to actually do the hard work of eliminating valuation killers, implementing meaningful systems, and increasing the reliability of future cash flows.

Second: Business Readiness for underperforming or undermanaged companies. If you don’t have a plan, you can’t understand why you’re not making the money you want. We have a proprietary integrated governance system that takes your data, extracts insights, presents you with decisions, describes actions for your team, and evaluates your results. Then the system does it again.

Our next offering will help teams save 4–8 weeks each year. What do you lose from that month (or two!) your teams aren’t able to make forward progress because they’re collecting old data? More on that later.

Expect more blog posts showing you how these systems work, new web pages with more details, and videos long and short for those who prefer watching to reading.
