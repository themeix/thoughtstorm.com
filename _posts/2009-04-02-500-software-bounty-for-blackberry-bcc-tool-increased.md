Now that Rim is [adding](<http://www.wsj.com/articles/SB123859655259678345>) its own [app store](<https://appworld.blackberry.com/webstore/?countrycode=US&lang=en>), titled “App World,” we’re renewing and increasing our earlier software bounty for a tool (plugin/patch/hack) that allows users of hosted BES to enable automatic BCC’ing of any address.

Our [original post](<https://www.thoughtstorm.com/software-bounty-250-for-blackberry-email-tweak/>) described the features and functions, with an original bounty of $250.

New Bounty: $500 (work-for-hire) and 40% of profits from App World (including recovery of initial $500 R&D).

Submissions go [here](<http://www.imetrick.com/>). I will be posting this on rent-a-coder as soon as I can get to it.
